## Key Features

### Visual Hierarchy
- **Hero**: Immediate impact with full-screen visuals
- **Category Gallery**: Early product discovery
- **Social Proof**: Testimonials and Instagram feed
- **Multiple CTAs**: Strategic placement throughout

### Content Flow
1. **Attention** → Hero with bold headline
2. **Interest** → Features and category gallery
3. **Desire** → Brand story, collections, testimonials
4. **Action** → Multiple CTA banners and newsletter

### Engagement Points
- 3 Major CTA Sections (Hero, Mid-page Banner, Newsletter)
- 2 Product Grids (New Arrivals, Top Demanded)
- Instagram Social Proof
- Customer Testimonials
- Interactive Category Gallery

### Mobile Optimization
- All sections responsive
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Optimized image loading
- Readable text sizes
- Collapsible grids
